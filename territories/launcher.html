Can't scan the QR code
Open your phone's camera app and point it at the QR code.
Or type the URL shown below the QR directly into Chrome or Safari.
Make sure you're using https:// — not http.
Page won't load on phone
Make sure the launcher is still running (this window open).
If the ngrok URL has changed, click Refresh and re-launch.
Try a different browser — Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone).
Camera won't start
Allow camera access when prompted.
On Android: Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
The URL must start with https:// — camera is blocked on plain http.
Can't find / scan a surface
Move slowly and point the camera at a flat, well-lit surface.
Floors, tables, and desks work best.
Avoid very dark rooms or plain white surfaces with no texture.
Scene placed in wrong spot / floating
Use the Reset button (top right of the AR view) to try again.
Walk back from where you placed the scene — move around to re-anchor it.
Scene is very laggy
Close other apps on your phone.
Large GIF files (over 2MB) will reduce performance.
Reduce the number of objects or GIF sizes for smoother results.
Vanadium / privacy browsers
Vanadium (GrapheneOS) blocks WebXR and camera APIs.
Use standard Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone) instead.
